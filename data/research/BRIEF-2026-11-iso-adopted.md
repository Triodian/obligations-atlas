# Research brief: ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Annex A and ISO/IEC 42005:2025 in the adopted tier

**13 November 2026 · development plan task ISO.8**

**Why.** A client that runs an AI management system to ISO/IEC 42001:2023 treats its Annex A controls as binding on itself, although the standard is voluntary in law. A matter brief for such a client should list the controls the client has adopted beside the legal instruments, so counsel and the client read them together. Which controls apply is the client's choice, recorded in its Statement of Applicability, so the records sit in a fourth tier, **adopted**, and the brief lists only those the client's Statement says apply.

**What.** One record for each of the 38 controls of Annex A (A.2.2 to A.10.4), each resting on the existing record for the standard as a whole (OBL-INT-ISO42001, expected tier), and one record for ISO/IEC 42005:2025 (AI system impact assessment). Each states the control number and short title as published, and a sentence in Triodian's words on what the control is about. The control text is not quoted: ISO sells the standard.

**Source.** The ISO catalogue pages for both standards (rank 3, standards body). The control numbers and titles match Table A.1 as listed by several secondary sources.

**For the reviewer.**
- Check each paraphrase against a licensed copy of ISO/IEC 42001:2023.
- Decide whether ISO/IEC 42005:2025, which is guidance, belongs here or in the expected tier.
- Nothing in a record says whether a client meets a control.
